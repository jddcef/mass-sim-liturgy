Spoken words

Pre-generated AI recordings play offline, with fixed character voices and six layered voices for congregation responses. Static Latin pronunciation is experimental (Latin phonemes with English-trained voices). Live mode uses your installed local voices and speaks congregation parts as one speaker. Latin is a partial starter pack, not a complete Missal. Missing translations are not spoken. Sung words are spoken, not chanted. Playback waits for audio to finish.

Church layout

Illustrative sanctuary arrangements within the same church shell, not named architectural plans. You can edit any preset afterwards.

X runs left to right; depth runs from the sanctuary's back wall towards the nave. Furniture, nearby movement destinations, vessels and cameras move together. Positions stay within the sanctuary; paths are schematic and do not provide collision avoidance. Layout is saved in this browser; export for a portable copy.

Celebration setup

Changes pause playback and restart the current step, retaining its place in the rite. Incense requires a server here; both kinds requires a deacon. Those options are disabled otherwise.
