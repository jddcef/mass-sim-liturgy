This shows a Sunday Mass in Ordinary Time with a Deacon, incense and servers, one of several forms the Missal allows (GIRM 115ff.). The rubrics are paraphrased, and only short responses are quoted. Postures follow GIRM 43; kneeling customs differ between Conferences of Bishops. Many options are not shown here, such as the Sprinkling Rite and other Eucharistic Prayers.
